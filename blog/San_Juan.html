San Juan

Aug 25, 2026 San Juan, Puerto Rico

I've been here for 4 days and never once I've heard about the Puerto Rican revolution. I took American History II merely weeks ago, yet not a word about the conflict was mentioned. I vaguely know that Vieques was a weapon test site, but I never understand the impact it has on local. Yet I called myself a traveler. Yet I was proud of being "civic literate" by taking a class with a Pulitzer winner journalist.

Why do I travel then?

A traveler goes out of their way to see the world. A traveler goes out to see what cannot be communicate by book or lecture. A traveler goes out to see what has been gatekeep away from them: A dark history that has been forgotten, or the Atlantic sunset on a castle while the wind blowing non-stop.

Atlantic sunset over El Morro, San Juan.
