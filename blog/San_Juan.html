Travel · Puerto Rico

San Juan

Aug 25, 2026 San Juan, Puerto Rico

I've been here for four days, and only now have I heard about the Puerto Rican uprisings. I took American History II merely weeks ago, yet not a word about them was mentioned. I vaguely knew that Vieques had been a weapons test site, but I never understood the impact it had on the people who live there. Yet I called myself a traveler. Yet I was proud of being "civically literate" for taking a class with a Pulitzer-winning journalist.

Why do I travel, then?

A traveler goes out of their way to see the world. A traveler goes out to see what cannot be communicated by a book or a lecture. A traveler goes out to see what has been kept from them: a dark history that has been forgotten, or the Atlantic sunset over a castle while the wind blows non-stop.

Atlantic sunset over El Morro, San Juan.
